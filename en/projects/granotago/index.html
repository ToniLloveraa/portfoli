GranotaGo

31 Oct 2025

GranotaGo is a casual, colorful game aimed at children aged 6 to 8 and developed in Unity, in which the player controls a frog that must move through dynamic scenes, avoiding obstacles and collecting points. It was created for 3Cat's Premis Ludi together with other CITM students.

Main features

  • Drag & drop mechanics
  • Colorful scenes
  • Scoring system

During development, I worked on programming the panel and block system. I was also in charge of testing, fixing and polishing the levels.
The project was presented at 3Cat's Premis Ludi and stood out for its look and smooth gameplay.
