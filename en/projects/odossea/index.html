Odossea

Feb 2026

Award winner at the 9th Gran CITM GameJam

Odossea is a cooperative platformer and puzzle game for two players. You throw and share mass to solve puzzles and clear platforms together: the characters can split up to work together on their odyssey. It is played with keyboard and mouse plus one gamepad, or with two gamepads.

Main features

  • Local co-op multiplayer for 2 players
  • Throw-and-share-mass mechanic
  • Interactive tutorial
  • Gamepad support

Developed as a team by TorratsGames for the 9th Gran CITM GameJam (January–February 2026), organized by Delegació CITM UPC. It ranked #1 in Gameplay in the jam's voting.
