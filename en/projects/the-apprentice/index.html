The Apprentice

05 Jun 2025

The Apprentice is a 2D platformer and adventure game in which the player plays a young apprentice who seeks to master ancient magic. It was developed with SDL2 and Tiled, combining a handcrafted art direction with precise level design.

Main features

  • Physics and collision system in C++
  • Tiled map integration
  • Basic AI for enemies and bosses

My main role was programming the logic for menus, transitions, levels, saving and loading, and UI. I also helped integrate art assets, programmed enemy AI and optimized the base engine's performance.
