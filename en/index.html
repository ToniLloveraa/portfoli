Estik

Role: programmer, UI programmer and technical artist

20264 peoplePremis Ludi 2026

About the project

Estik is an action and skill video game inspired by field hockey in which the player must complete a series of dynamic levels. The goal of each stage is to guide the ball to the goal, overcoming a wide variety of environmental obstacles and challenges that test the player's precision, trajectory calculation and mental agility. It was created for the Premis Ludi 2026 by 3Cat.

Main features

  • Dynamic levels with environmental obstacles
  • Guide the ball to the goal
  • Tests precision and trajectory calculation

My work

Four-person team project. My role was programmer, UI programmer and technical artist.

Team

  • Marc Blanquez Padilla: programmer, 3D artist and level designer (GitHub)
  • Martí Mira Vázquez: level designer, UI artist and 3D artist (GitHub)
  • Oscar Alonso Camenforte: programmer, sound programmer and level designer (GitHub)
  • Toni Llovera Roca: programmer, UI programmer and technical artist (GitHub)

Odossea

Socialpoint Best Technology Award – 9th Gran CITM Game Jam
Feb 2026Game jam2 players

About the project

Odossea is a cooperative platformer and puzzle game for two players. You throw and share mass to solve puzzles and clear platforms together: the characters can split up to work together on their odyssey. It is played with keyboard and mouse plus one gamepad, or with two gamepads.

Main features

  • Local co-op multiplayer for 2 players
  • Throw-and-share-mass mechanic
  • Interactive tutorial
  • Gamepad support

Development

Developed as a team by TorratsGames for the 9th Gran CITM GameJam (January–February 2026), organized by Delegació CITM UPC. It ranked #1 in Gameplay in the jam's voting.

Son Of Ithaca

Nominee: Best Academic Game – IndieDevDay · SAGA Game Fest 2026
Jul 202620+ peopleWindows

About the project

Son of Ithaca is an action-adventure game based on The Odyssey, where you play as Telemachus searching for his father, Odysseus. With the help of Athena, goddess of wisdom, travel to a mysterious island and explore it to gain new powers from the gods in the form of equippable masks; then open the portal to the Underworld and face the final challenges.

Main features

  • 3D action-adventure based on The Odyssey
  • Equippable masks with the powers of the gods
  • Exploration of a mysterious island and a portal to the Underworld

Development

Project developed as a team by Nobody Games Studio, with over 20 people. Free for Windows; roughly one hour long.

Need For Beer

Nov 2024C++1–2 players

About the project

Need For Beer is a 1 or 2 player racing game with an emphasis on the physical simulation of vehicle movement. It was developed collaboratively in a GitHub repository with classmates, as part of a study of physics mechanics applied to video games.

Main features

  • Vehicle physics simulation (forces, friction, collisions)
  • Dynamic circuits and crash-and-bounce mechanics
  • Custom keyboard controls, turbo system and advanced braking

My work

In this project, my main role was designing and implementing the lap systems, as well as integrating the turbo system, all the UI and the main art. I also helped structure the GitHub repository and optimize for stable performance.
